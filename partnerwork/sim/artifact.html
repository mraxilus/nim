The body sim

Two torso cylinders seen from above, a head and neck stacked on each, and arms that are rope: inextensible, anchored at the shoulders, tied at the joined hands. Turn a body and the rope winds onto it — where the solver puts it, not where a drawing would like it. Keep turning and it runs out. Nothing here stands for anything; it is the model, drawn.

Below the plan is the same state from the side, which is what settles whether a rope clears a head and which of two crossing ropes is over. Every turn is replayed from rest, so what you see depends on where the sliders are and not on how you dragged them there.
